Demonstrated: recorded conversation decisions produce traceable point annotations in an inspected development scene. Research direction: task-driven visual observation for large 3D inputs. Net gains over geometry baselines and observation/token efficiency still require controlled comparisons.
What does the height view retain?
XY determines pixel position and Z affects brightness. A top view keeps the highest visible record at each pixel; depth and point mappings are stored locally. One projection does not retain all overlapping height layers. Complementary views supply additional evidence.


WHO DOES WHAT
Geometry supplies evidence.
The observer interprets it.
The model receives visual observations. The original cloud stays with the executor. Observation volume and precise point identity are separated; token/accuracy trade-offs have not yet been measured.

Render · Map · Verify · Export

Evidence scope and current limits
This is a conversation-vision prototype and an inspected development-scene result. Fresh annotation needs a capable visual observer. Playback and action replay do not invoke a model. Unattended repeatability, held-out generalization, independent wire identity and endpoint/length completeness remain unverified.
